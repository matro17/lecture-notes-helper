Artificial Intelligence and Neural Networks

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science dedicated to creating systems capable of performing creative tasks that traditionally require human intelligence.

Key Lecture Points:

  • Machine Learning (ML): An AI method where a system learns from data datasets to identify hidden patterns.
  • Neural Networks: Mathematical models that mimic the structure of biological neurons in the human brain.
  • Deep Learning (DL): The use of multi-layered neural networks for complex pattern recognition and text generation.

Key terms:

Frontend, Algorithm, Training Dataset, Connection Weights.
